HOW IT WORKS

A simple support flow.

The VILS Center keeps student/device identity centralized while giving each role the tools it needs.

01

Students

Sign in, choose a service, enter Student ID, verify the roster/device card, and submit.

02

Teachers

Choose student or teacher device. Student-device tickets use the same exact-ID lookup workflow.

03

TechTeam

Work the repair queue, track loaners and shipping, and use the activity log for operational visibility.

04
